Master the 5-Day SSB
Evaluation Pipeline

Detailed tactical roadmap breakdown covering Stage-I Screening, Psychology Batteries, Group Testing Officer (GTO) tasks, and Interview Technique.

01
Day 1

Screening Test

Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) verbal & non-verbal tests followed by PPDT picture story writing and group discussion.

02
Day 2

Psychology Batteries

Projective tests including TAT (12 slides), WAT (60 words), SRT (60 situations), and Self Description (SD).

03
Day 3

GTO Series Part I

Group Discussions, Military Planning Exercise, Progressive Group Task, Half Group Task, and Snake Race.

04
Day 4

GTO Series Part II & Interview

Individual Obstacles, Command Task, Final Group Task, and personal interview interaction with the IO.

05
Day 5

Board Conference

Final joint board evaluation by all officers followed by merit recommendation results declaration.
