Officer Selection

The SSB Journey

Understand the five major stages of the Services Selection Board evaluation process.

  1. 01

    Stage I: Screening

    OIR (Officer Intelligence Rating) tests, PPDT story writing, and group discussion stage.

  2. 02

    Stage II: Psychology

    TAT, WAT, SRT, and Self-Description (SD) psychometric battery assessment.

  3. 03

    Stage III: GTO Tasks

    Outdoor obstacle courses, progressive group tasks, group discussions, and leadership tests.

  4. 04

    Stage IV: Personal Interview

    In-depth personal interaction with the Interviewing Officer assessing Officer Like Qualities.

  5. 05

    Stage V: Board Conference

    Final evaluation panel interaction and recommendation decision by all assessors.
